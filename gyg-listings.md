## Ground rules (apply to every listing)

- **Driver only, no guide.** Under Italian law only a licensed guide can give
  commentary, so the listing is a *private sightseeing* tour: no "guided",
  "expert guide", "hear the story", "learn", "discover the secret".
- **Private** — "just for you and your group"; set the option as private
  (per-group price) in the backend, not shared.
- Backend: option must **not** say "Live tour guide". Not included: "Tour guide".
- "Pass" / "stop for photos", never "visit" (implies entry; Pantheon is
  ticketed since 2023).
- Pickup/drop-off "wherever you wish" → limit to **central Rome**, set
  "Pickup included" + draw the pickup area on GYG's map.
- No gelato/coffee/drink unless Best Way actually pays for it on every booking.

## Day tour

**Title**
Rome: Private Golf Cart Sightseeing Tour with Hotel Pickup

**Short description (option A — with pickup)**
See Rome's top sights on a private golf cart ride, with pickup and drop-off
wherever you wish. Pass the Colosseum, Pantheon and Trevi Fountain, with photo
stops and free time in Trastevere.

**Short description (option B)**
See Rome's highlights by day on a private golf cart tour just for you and your
group. Pass the Colosseum, Trevi Fountain, Spanish Steps and more, with stops
for photos along the way.

**Full description**
Discover the highlights of Rome on a private sightseeing tour by golf cart,
just for you and your group. Your driver picks you up wherever you wish in
central Rome and drops you off wherever you like at the end — no meeting points
to find, no strangers, and the tour runs at your own pace.

Ride past the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill, then loop around the Colosseum
and the Arch of Constantine, with stops for photos from the best angles.
Continue to the Circus Maximus, then cross the river to Trastevere, Rome's most
charming neighborhood, with free time to stroll its cobbled streets and Piazza
Santa Maria in Trastevere.

Finish with stops at the Pantheon, the Trevi Fountain and the Spanish Steps,
where you can take in the view from the top of the steps. The easiest way to
see the Eternal City's icons in one ride, without the long walks and without
the crowds.

**Itinerary**
1. Your driver picks you up at your hotel or any location of your choice in
   central Rome, and you board your private golf cart.
2. Roman Forum and Palatine Hill — panoramic view over the ruins (from
   outside: Via dei Fori Imperiali / Campidoglio — the cart can't reach the
   Via Sacra).
3. Full loop around the Colosseum and the Arch of Constantine, photo stops.
4. Circus Maximus.
5. Trastevere — free time to stroll, Piazza Santa Maria in Trastevere.
6. Pantheon — admire the dome from the piazza.
7. Trevi Fountain — toss a coin and make your wish to return to Rome; drop-off
   wherever you like in central Rome.

**Included:** private golf cart with driver; pickup and drop-off in central Rome.
**Not included:** tour guide; entry to monuments; food and drinks.

## Night tour

**Title**
Rome: Private Nighttime Golf Cart Sightseeing Tour

**Short description**
See Rome's landmarks lit up at night on a private golf cart ride. Pass the
Trevi Fountain, Pantheon, Spanish Steps and more, and enjoy a panoramic view of
the city from Gianicolo Hill.

**Full description**
Climb aboard a private electric golf cart and see Rome at night, when its
monuments light up and the city takes on a unique charm. Quiet and agile, the
cart slips through alleys and squares where large buses can't go — a relaxed,
romantic experience for couples, families and anyone looking for a different
view of the Eternal City.

Your driver picks you up wherever you wish in central Rome and takes you past
the city's icons: Piazza Venezia and the Altare della Patria, the Imperial
Forums, the Jewish Ghetto, the Pantheon, the Trevi Fountain, the Spanish Steps,
Via del Corso and Piazza del Popolo, with stops for photos along the way.

Then head up to the Janiculum Hill for a breathtaking panoramic view over the
illuminated city. A flexible 2-hour tour just for you and your group, ending
wherever you like in central Rome.
